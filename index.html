The SpotLIGHT Standards

Community Rules

✅ Allowed Content

  • 🏔️ Utah & local content: Wasatch Front culture, neighborhoods, spots
  • ☕ Businesses: Local shops, cafes, gyms, dining & services
  • 📸 Creators: Photographers, filmmakers, influencers, podcasters
  • 🎟️ Events: Concerts, pop-ups, markets, nightlife & gatherings
  • 🎨 Art: Murals, galleries, tattoo artists, ceramics, sculptors
  • 🎵 Music: Local bands, releases, producers & DJs
  • 👗 Fashion: Boutiques, vintage drops, street style & designers
  • 📢 News & announcements: Local updates & openings
  • 💬 Community discussions: Local recommendations & questions

❌ Strictly Prohibited

  • 🚫 Spam: Bots, repetitive spam, or fake links
  • ⚠️ Scams: Fraud, impersonation, or financial schemes
  • ⚖️ Illegal content: Unlawful activity, piracy or contraband
  • 🛑 Harassment: Bullying, threats, or defamation
  • 🛑 Hate: Hate speech, slurs, or discrimination
  • 🔞 Explicit sexual content: Adult or NSFW media
  • 🚨 Dangerous/violent content: Violence or physical threats
